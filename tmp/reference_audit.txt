FULL: ABSTRACT || The rapid advancement of audio generation technologies has enabled the creation of || highly realistic synthetic environmental sounds, raising serious concerns in areas such as || audio forensics, surveillance, and media authentication. Unlike speech-based deepfake || detection, environmental audio presents unique challenges due to its unstructured, || non-stationary, and highly dynamic nature. || This project proposes a multi-perspective deep learning framework for detecting synthetic || environmental audio by combining spectral and temporal analysis. The system employs a || dual-branch architecture consisting of an attention-enhanced spectral model inspired by || AASIST and a temporal modeling branch based on State-Space Models (SSMs) to || capture long-range dependencies. An attention-based fusion mechanism integrates || features from both branches to enhance detection performance. The model is trained and || evaluated on the EnvSDD dataset under both balanced and imbalanced conditions. || Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed approach outperforms baseline || models, achieving higher accuracy and lower Equal Error Rate (EER). || Keywords: || Audio Forensics, Environmental Audio, Deepfake Detection, Spectral Analysis, Temporal || Modeling, Attention-Based Fusion, Deep Learning, Dual-Branch Architecture, || State-Space Model || 4
